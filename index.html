The Process

From submission to indexed record.

1

Submit

Share your manuscript, patent draft or thesis through the editorial desk with your discipline and target track.

2

Review

Independent subject experts assess originality, methodology and contribution under a blind review process.

3

Revise

Authors receive structured feedback and a clear window to refine the work before final evaluation.

4

Publish

Approved work is formatted, assigned a reference record and released with an author certificate.

5

Disseminate

Published work is indexed and listed on the live Publications page for global visibility.
